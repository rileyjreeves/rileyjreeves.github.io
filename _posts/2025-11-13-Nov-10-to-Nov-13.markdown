# Monday, November 10th 

pulled an all-nighter and almost felt my heart flutter out of my chest, all for a meeting with my PI which I always stress about and which always goes fine. 
As Dalyaro said, pattern recognition doesn't apply to such primal fears as presenting a powerpoint. Still working through the halloween candy. The leaves are prettier every day. 

# Tuesday, November 11th 

cold as the devil. The pile of clothes and clutter on my bed has now accumulated enough density to mimic the warmth of a human body. 
My shoulder's been bugging me but it probably has nothing to do with the weird sound my neck makes sometimes. 

# Wednesday, November 12th 

went to the gas station and bought gummy worms and cigarettes. said I was going to clean my room and didn't. Preoccupied with the fact of this being my last fall on campus as an undergrad.
I guess I could still flunk. 

# Thursday, November 13th 

sometimes my back feels like icy-hot even when I haven't put any icy-hot on. surely this is a good sign of my impeccable nerve and muscle health. In the dining hall on lunch break from lab, 
a girl I met freshman year sat at a table beside me. Crazy how in situations like these the 3.5 odd years collapse and all of a sudden it's August 2022 and we are freshmen in our environmental-
advocacy-themed orientation group, wide-eyed and grinning and trembling. Feels like no time has passed at all. And yet. 
This morning I showered in Nathan home after we smoked behind Edens dorm at 7am. I used his towel but not his toothbrush. Before all that, I took Irma to the airport, and watched the sun rise over i-40.

title:  "For Those Who Seek Routine Vehicle Maintenance" 
date:   2026-03-28 14:27:16 -0400
categories: poetry
---
I lost years of my life   
To Tim of the Leith Honda Service desk   
  
I told him I had a thousand miles to drive   
He said that’s nothing, try California and back   
  
My rotors, my calipers, my oil, my strife   
All uncovered by warranty gone slack   
  
Salesmen, mechanics, dealers contrive   
To be one stabbing pain in my neck
